Runs our Stage 1 success criteria exactly as written and keeps the results for the report.

Success criterion 3: performance

With five concurrent users on a stable connection of at least 10 Mbps, at least 19 out of 20 menu loads and 19 out of 20 order submissions complete within three seconds.

Each order submission is timed from sending the request to receiving the saved order, which is when the confirmation appears. Test orders use the name "Load test" and are flagged as test data in the database, so they never mix with the analysis of real orders.

Success criterion 2: correct end-to-end handling

All 20 scripted test orders appear exactly once in the staff dashboard with the correct items, quantities and totals. For each order, the customer’s status page matches the staff’s saved status after refreshing.

Success criterion 1: first-time users

At least four out of five first-time test users can place a two-item pickup order without assistance in two minutes or less on a 360 px-wide phone screen, with no horizontal scrolling.

Open on the tester’s phone. Give them the task card, press Start the task and stay silent. The timer stops by itself when the order is confirmed. Write down whether they needed help.
